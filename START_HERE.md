# Handover: West Midlands measles modelling project

The archive bundles the files from the latest consolidated editorial release, plus `PARAMETERS_AND_HANDOVER.md` describing fixed and estimated quantities exactly as implemented. Start with `report/west_midlands_measles_research_report.pdf`, then read `PARAMETERS_AND_HANDOVER.md`, `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`, and `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`. The seven scripts in `analysis/` (00–06) are the computational source of the results; `PROFILE_LIKELIHOOD_FINDINGS.md` summarises the identifiability and mechanism results and how they were verified.

The manuscript remains a research draft for critical review. It uses the UKHSA West Midlands 2023–2024 measles outbreak as an empirical case study, and compares a constant-transmission SEIR model against a one-change model whose change date was chosen retrospectively. Interpretation of estimated epidemiological parameters is limited by the observation proxy and structural non-identifiability.
